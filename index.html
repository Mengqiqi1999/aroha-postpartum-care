What We Offer

Two ways we support new families

Whether you need hands-on postpartum support or want to become a confinement carer yourself, we're here to help.

Confinement Nanny Placement

We match vetted, experienced NZ confinement nannies with families in Australia for live-in or live-out postpartum support, newborn care and traditional recovery practices.

Caregiver Training Program

A structured training pathway for anyone in NZ wanting to become a professional postpartum carer, covering newborn care, nutrition, and cross-cultural client care.

Ongoing Support

From first enquiry to placement and beyond, our team stays in touch with both families and carers to make sure every arrangement goes smoothly.

Our Story

Rooted in New Zealand care, trusted across Australia

We started this agency to bridge two things new parents need most: hands they can trust, and knowledge that respects tradition. Every nanny in our network is trained, background-checked and supported by our team on both sides of the Tasman.
