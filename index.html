What We Offer

How we support families

Whether you need hands-on care from the newborn stage through to school age, support for an elderly family member, or want to become a professional caregiver yourself, we're here to help.

Postpartum & Newborn Care

Support for the postpartum period and newborn stage, including mother-and-baby care, overnight help where needed, and confinement meal (月子餐) preparation. Our care approach draws on Taiwan confinement-centre methods and traditional Chinese medicine-informed recovery concepts.

Long-term Nanny & Childcare

Longer-term nanny and childcare support for babies from around 2 months through to preschool or school age, with flexible live-in, live-out, day or overnight arrangements.

Elder Companion Care

Companionship and gentle day-to-day support for older family members, such as conversation, errands, simple outings and regular visits. This is positioned as companion support, not medical or high-dependency care.

Caregiver Training Program

A practical training pathway for people in New Zealand who want to become professional postpartum caregivers, covering newborn care, postpartum nutrition, family communication and client-home service etiquette.

Our Story

Rooted in New Zealand care, serving families across NZ and Australia

Joyful was created to connect families with trustworthy hands and care knowledge that respects tradition. Our founding team brings years of hands-on experience from Taiwanese postpartum care centres, combined with a practical background in traditional Chinese bodywork, therapeutic massage and postpartum recovery support. Our support does not stop at birth — we help families from the newborn stage through preschool childcare, and we also provide gentle elder companion support. Every caregiver in our network is trained, reviewed and supported by our team across New Zealand and Australia.

FAQ

Common questions

Do your nannies speak English?+

Our coordination team supports English and Chinese communication, and many caregivers are bilingual in English and Mandarin. Let us know your family's preference and we'll match accordingly.

How do I apply to become a 月嫂?+

Get in touch via the contact form below and our team will walk you through the training and application process, including course content, placement preparation and suitable service arrangements.

Which areas do you serve?+

We support families in cities and towns across New Zealand and Australia, including arrangements within NZ and between NZ and Australia.

How far in advance should I book?+

There's no fixed minimum — but our most experienced caregivers are matched quickly and can be booked out, so we recommend reaching out as soon as you know your due date or start date. Once you've met a caregiver you're happy with, we recommend confirming with a deposit and signed agreement promptly, as popular caregivers are often booked by other families in the meantime.

Are service fees published on the website?+

Service fees are sensitive and are not published on the website for now. They vary depending on care type, location, schedule, live-in or live-out needs, and duration, so we discuss them after understanding your family's situation.
